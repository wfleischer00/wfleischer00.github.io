Mid-February in Snowmass: days around 30°F on the village slopes and colder up top, nights in the single digits, dry powder most weeks, and strong sun at altitude. Pack layers, goggles and sunscreen. Snowmass Village sits at 8,200 feet, so drink plenty of water the first two days and go easy on the wine at Saturday's dinner. Each day's chip refreshes itself with a live forecast once the trip is 16 days out.

American nonstop DFW → Aspen (ASE)to book
The pick from American's five nonstops that day: depart 12:05 PM, land 2:03 PM Mountain, about three hours. It's a regional jet with a small first-class cabin. Sit on the right going in: the approach up the Roaring Fork Valley passes close to the peaks. Earlier (8:30, 10:31) and later (2:35, 6:45 PM) nonstops cost the same. Aspen is a mountain airport where wind or snow can divert flights, so the midday flight leaves the most room to recover the same day.

Bosq, Aspen's only Michelin-starred restaurant (one star, 2025), 20–25 minutes down to town. Its 5-course menu is $215 and the 9-course Chef's Tasting $295 per person. Saturday the 13th had only four tables left on Nov 21 (6:15, 6:30, 8:00, 8:15), and Bosq is closed Sundays, so it can't be the Valentine's dinner. Take 8:00 if you'd rather land, settle in and change first. Snowcat dinner on the mountainTock, early Jan🍽 Reserve
Snowcat dinner at The Cabin, on Snowmass Mountain: a heated snowcat up the dark slopes to a five-course dinner, $229.49 a person all-in, prepaid and non-refundable.
