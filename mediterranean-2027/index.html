May in the Mediterranean: highs in the low 70s from Barcelona to Sicily and near 80 in Athens, cool evenings on deck, and very little rain. The sun sets after 8 PM everywhere, so the sailaways come in daylight. On Etna's summit it is about 30 degrees colder, so bring a warm layer. Each day's chip shows a May normal until the trip is inside the 16-day forecast window (around Apr 20), then refreshes itself whenever the page opens. Times are local: Barcelona to Sicily is 7 hours ahead of Dallas, and Mykonos and Athens are 8.

Getting aroundPorts, drivers and all-aboardread before each port
All aboard
An hour before sailing
Virgin's own page sets all-aboard an hour before departure in Barcelona and posts each port's time at the gangway and in the app. Every plan here has you back an hour before that. The ship waits only for its own Shore Things, never for a private driver, so keep the margin.
Drivers
Pier pickups
Every port day uses a private driver who meets you at the terminal with a name sign. Send each one the ship's name and the berth the evening before, and get prices in writing. Italy's licensed hire cars (NCC) may enter the restricted old-town zones where taxis and private cars can't.
Barcelona
Taxis and the Moll Adossat
Black-and-yellow taxis are metered and take cards. There's a €4.60 supplement to or from the cruise pier, and the airport–Moll Adossat fare is a fixed €46. Short hops around the Eixample run €10–15. Pickpockets work Passeig de Gràcia, the Gothic Quarter and the Sagrada Família queues.
Rome
Train as a fallback
Regional FL5 trains run every 30 minutes from Civitavecchia to Roma San Pietro (50–60 minutes, €4.60). On strike days only 6–9 AM and 6–9 PM are guaranteed, which is why the plan uses a car.
Athens
Metro, taxis and the airport
Yellow taxis are plentiful and can be called through Uber or FreeNow. The fixed fare between the center and the airport was set to rise to €50 by day (€65 from midnight to 5 AM). Metro Line 3 runs from Syntagma to the airport in about 40 minutes, but only with carry-ons.
Money
Euros everywhere ashore
Cards work almost everywhere. Carry €50–100 in small notes for kiosks, ferries, tips and the €1.50 restrooms at the Stadium. Tipping is modest: 5–10% at good restaurants and about 10% for a full-day driver.
Entry
Passports and the EU's new border system
Passports must be valid at least three months past May 18. The Entry/Exit System takes fingerprints and a photo on your first entry in Barcelona. Check in January whether ETIAS pre-registration has launched.
