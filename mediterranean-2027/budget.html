Fine Hotels + Resorts at El Palace and the Grande BretagnePrepay each on Amex Travel with a different personal Platinum: daily breakfast for two, an upgrade and noon check-in when available, a guaranteed 4 PM checkout (useful on embarkation day), and the $300 Jan–Jun hotel credit on each card.
Which card pays for the suiteThe Platinum, through Amex Travel (800-525-3355): it earns 1x on cruises, and the Cruise & Yacht Collection adds 1 more, so 2x (about 21,800 points on the $10,910 quote). It also brings $100–300 onboard credit, one amenity, and the Platinum's trip cancellation and interruption cover. The Green earns 3x on cruises booked through Amex Travel (about 32,700 points), but it gets none of the cruise benefits or that cover. The extra point is worth about $120 at 1.1¢, less than the credit alone. Ask Amex whether Virgin's own $300 onboard credit carries over; if it doesn't, compare the two credits before choosing.
