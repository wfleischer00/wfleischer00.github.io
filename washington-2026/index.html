10:00 AM
Library of Congress — Thomas Jefferson Buildingbooked ✓
The most beautiful room in Washington — the Great Hall's mosaics and the Main Reading Room overlook, plus the Gutenberg Bible. Your pass is for the opening slot so the halls are still quiet; airport-style screening at the door, and don't arrive before the pass time. Ninety minutes is plenty.
11:30 AM
Walk the Mall, lunch on the way📍 Directions~1.8 mi
Cross to the Capitol grounds, down the west-front terraces, and onto the Mall at 3rd Street — then west along the gravel with the museums on either side: the American Indian museum, Air & Space, Natural History, American History, and the Washington Monument at the far end. Forty minutes of walking plus whatever stops you like; the Monument is the goal, not the rule. Lunch on the route: the Mitsitam Café inside the American Indian museum (4th & Independence, 11–3 — Native cooking by region, the Mall's best museum lunch), or Fiesta DC's food stalls one block north on Pennsylvania between 3rd and 7th — pupusas and arepas, 11 to 7. Rain plan: the museums themselves; Natural History and American History are dry for as long as you need.

Paying
Tap a card, or SmarTrip in Apple Wallet🎟 Set up
Every faregate takes a contactless credit card or Apple Pay — tap in and tap out with the same card, one card per rider. Smoother still: add a SmarTrip to each iPhone's Wallet before you leave Dallas ($2 card, $4 minimum load) and turn on Express Transit so it works without Face ID. Load $10 each; the weekend's rides for two run about $25. Day passes don't pay off.
