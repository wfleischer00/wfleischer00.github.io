The Convocation

William is made a Fellow of the American College of Surgeons — then a capital weekend for two
Sep 25 – 28, 2026 · 3 nightsConrad Washington, DC · CityCenter
⌂ TripsItineraryBudget
The shape of it. Fly in Friday midday on AA 1177, three nights at the Conrad — a six-minute walk from the convention center. Saturday is the day: the Library of Congress in the morning, William robes at 4, the Convocation runs 5:45–7 in Hall B, and The Capital Welcome Reception follows until 9. The rest of the congress we skip in favor of the city — the Archives and the National Gallery, a walk down the Mall, Arlington, the Smithsonian on its anniversary weekend, the White House from next door, and three booked tables. The city runs on the Metro this trip: the airport station is a covered bridge from your terminal, the Conrad sits between two stations, and no fare tops three dollars. One wrinkle — the Yellow Line is closed Saturday and Sunday for bridge work, so weekend rides go Blue, Green or Red. Forecast is cool and damp (upper 60s Friday sliding to the 50s, drizzle most days), so umbrellas in the bag. Sunset ≈ 7:00 PM. Times are local to where you are — Central through DFW, Eastern from wheels-down on.

Your stations
Metro Center · Gallery Place · Mt Vernon Sq
Metro Center (11th & G, 0.3 mi) is the all-weekend workhorse — Red, Blue, Orange and Silver, untouched by the track work. Gallery Place (9th & G, 0.25 mi) is Red, Green and Yellow — the airport line on weekdays. Mt Vernon Sq (7th & M, 0.4 mi) is Green and Yellow and exits onto the convention center block.
Paying
Tap a card, or SmarTrip in Apple Wallet🎟 Set up
Every faregate takes a contactless credit card or Apple Pay — tap in and tap out with the same card, one card per rider. Smoother with luggage in hand: add a SmarTrip to each iPhone's Wallet before you leave Dallas ($2 card, $4 minimum load) and turn on Express Transit so it works without Face ID. Load $15 each; the whole weekend for two runs about $35. Day passes don't pay off.
Fares
Under three dollars, every ride
Weekdays are distance-based — the airport runs are about $2.80 each, downtown hops $2.25. Weekends everything is $2.25–2.50. Ride times are short: the airport is 14 minutes, Arlington 10, the Capitol 9.
This weekend
Yellow Line closed Sat–Sunre-check Thu
Bridge and track work shuts the Yellow Line both weekend days; it runs normally Friday and Monday, so both airport legs are unaffected. On the weekend, Blue trains still reach the airport and Arlington (signed Franconia-Springfield or Huntington) and should run every 6 minutes downtown, as they did the weekend before; Green covers the Mall's east end and Mt Vernon Sq. Silver is trimmed too, which touches nothing on this plan. Metro posts the detailed weekend advisory midweek — glance at wmata.com on Thursday, and again Sunday night for Monday's airport run.
Hours
Late trains, early trains
First trains 5 AM weekdays and 6 AM weekends; last trains 2 AM Friday and Saturday nights, midnight Sunday and Monday. Nothing on this plan comes near either end.
Crowds
Capital One Arena sits on top of Gallery Place
Capitals preseason Friday at 7, Gorillaz Saturday at 7, Charli xcx Monday at 7:30 — 7th & F is mobbed 6–7:30 and 10–11 those nights. Use the 9th & G entrance or Metro Center, and walk rather than ride through Penn Quarter at those hours.
