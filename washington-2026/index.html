The Convocation

William is made a Fellow of the American College of Surgeons — then a capital weekend for two
Sep 25 – 28, 2026 · 3 nightsConrad Washington, DC · CityCenter
⌂ TripsItineraryBudget
The shape of it. Fly in Friday midday on AA 1177, three nights at the Conrad — a six-minute walk from the convention center, so Saturday needs no car at all. Saturday is the day: William robes at 4, the Convocation runs 5:45–7 in Hall B, and The Capital Welcome Reception follows until 9. The rest of the congress we skip in favor of the city — monuments at dusk, the Library of Congress, the Smithsonian, Arlington, the White House from next door, and three good tables. Forecast is cool and damp (upper 60s Friday sliding to the 50s, drizzle most days), so the plan leans on marble halls with an umbrella in the bag. Sunset ≈ 7:00 PM. Times are local to where you are — Central through DFW, Eastern from wheels-down on.
